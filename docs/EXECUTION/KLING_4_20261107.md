# Kling 4.0 rollout — 2026-11-07

## Outcome and acceptance

Add the reviewed `kling-4.0` native video contract without changing existing
models, running a paid generation, or enabling it for partners. On 2026-11-08
the operator approved retail at procurement plus 20%; the model remains draft
until a successful live smoke is recorded.

Acceptance covers the exact native request shape, 3–30 second duration, 720p and
1080p tiers, text/reference ratios, 10 image and 5 video references (15 total),
no audio or frame references, optional generated audio, separate video-input
pricing, idempotent queue submission, confirmed-usage settlement, public RU/EN
reference material and a scoped repeatable database registration.

## Evidence and decisions

- Source reviewed: the public Argolink catalog endpoint
  `GET /api/catalog/v1/models?page_size=100` on 2026-11-08, revision
  `8b23c784088bce6f`.
- Procurement: 0.1256/0.188 USD per billed second for 720p/1080p. The live
  `reference_video_multiplier` is 0.6, producing exact input-video rates of
  0.07536/0.1128 USD per billed second.
- The approved retail formula is `procurement USD * current persisted RUB/USDT
  * 1.20`, rounded half-up to 0.01 RUB. At a 100 RUB/USDT example rate this is
  15.07/22.56 RUB per second for default mode and 9.04/13.54 RUB per second for
  `video_input`. The registration uses the production FX policy at apply time.
- `video_input` is a distinct pricing mode because its procurement and partner
  price must be snapshotted independently from prompt/image-only generations.
- The existing provider adapter, durable queue, idempotency, polling, protected
  content delivery and usage settlement are reused. Provider identities and raw
  task IDs remain internal.
- The registration command creates or updates the four reviewed price rows and
  four provider capability rows while the model stays draft. It never marks a
  smoke successful or enables access.
